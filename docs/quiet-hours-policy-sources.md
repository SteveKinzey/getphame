# Quiet-hours policy sources

## CAN-SPAM scope and timing distinction

The Federal Trade Commission's **CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business** lists commercial-email obligations including accurate header information, non-deceptive subject lines, advertising identification, a valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out mechanism, and honoring opt-outs within ten business days. The guide does **not** state a time-of-day sending prohibition.

Accordingly, Get Phame's **8:00 PM–8:00 AM business-local quiet period** is implemented as a customer-respect and deliverability best practice, not represented as a CAN-SPAM time-of-day mandate. The product still requires the business's physical address for local-time resolution and should preserve applicable CAN-SPAM requirements separately.

Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business (accessed 2026-08-01)
